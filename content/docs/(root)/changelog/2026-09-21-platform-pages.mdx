---
title: September 21, 2026 - Six Platform Pages
description: Scan now shows how your link looks on Google, Microsoft Teams, Reddit and Instagram, and Bluesky and Notion get pages of their own.
date: 2026-09-21
---

Six platforms got a page of their own in [Scan](/scan) today, each showing a
single preview instead of the full wall of cards, with the checks, the findings
and enough writing about that platform to be worth landing on from a search.
Four of them are new to Scan:

- [Google](/scan/google) — the search result built from the title tag and meta
  description rather than the Open Graph pair, contributed by
  [@kuldeeepy](https://github.com/kuldeeepy).
- [Microsoft Teams](/scan/teams) — the card under the message, one of the few
  surfaces that shows `og:site_name`, contributed by
  [@MayurK-cmd](https://github.com/MayurK-cmd).
- [Reddit](/scan/reddit) — the feed tile, where the headline is the
  submitter's and only the image is yours, contributed by
  [@MayurK-cmd](https://github.com/MayurK-cmd).
- [Instagram](/scan/instagram) — the DM bubble, the only place an Instagram
  link unfurls at all, contributed by
  [@MayurK-cmd](https://github.com/MayurK-cmd).

The other two, added on September 19, now have pages too:

- [Bluesky](/scan/bluesky) — the card attached to a post at compose time,
  contributed by [@MayurK-cmd](https://github.com/MayurK-cmd).
- [Notion](/scan/notion) — the bookmark block, text first with the image as a
  thumbnail on the right, contributed by
  [@AbhinRustagi](https://github.com/AbhinRustagi).
