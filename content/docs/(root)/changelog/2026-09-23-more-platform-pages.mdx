description: Scan now shows how your link looks on Threads, LINE, Google Chat, TikTok, WeChat, iMessage, Tumblr and Nextdoor.

---

Eight more platforms joined [Scan](/scan) today, each with its own page showing
a single preview instead of the full wall of cards, with the checks, the
findings and enough writing about that platform to be worth landing on from a
search:

- [Threads](/scan/threads) — the card under a post, image on top with the
  domain and title below.
- [LINE](/scan/line) — the chat bubble, full-width image over a bold title, one
  line of description and the domain.
- [Google Chat](/scan/google-chat) — the small card under the message, title
  and description beside a thumbnail.
- [TikTok](/scan/tiktok) — the compact in-app card for DMs and comments, a small
  thumbnail beside the title and domain.
- [WeChat](/scan/wechat) — the chat card, title across the top with the
  description and a square thumbnail beneath.
- [iMessage](/scan/imessage) — the rounded link bubble, image filling the top
  and the title over the site name.
- [Tumblr](/scan/tumblr) — the dashboard link post, image above the title,
  description and domain.
- [Nextdoor](/scan/nextdoor) — the post link card, large image above the title,
  description and domain.

All eight were contributed by [@MayurK-cmd](https://github.com/MayurK-cmd).
